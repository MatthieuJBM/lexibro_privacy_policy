# Lexibro – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 17.11.2026

Welcome to Lexibro! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

## 1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. All data, including your sources, words, review schedule, settings and custom languages, is stored locally on your device using Isar, an offline-first database.

## 2. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage: All data you enter into the app is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your data.

Export Feature: When you export, the app creates a JSON file in a temporary directory and hands it to the system share sheet. You choose the destination app or location, and you are solely responsible for the security and integrity of the exported file from that point on.

PDF Feature: The app can create PDF files of your words. Like backups, they are written to a temporary directory and handed to the system share sheet; we have no access to them, and you are solely responsible for them from that point on.

Import Feature: The app can import data from files with a valid JSON format. Importing modified files is supported only if they comply with the app's required JSON structure.

Device Backups: Your device's operating system (for example Google or iCloud backup) may include the app's data in its own backups, according to your device settings. We have no access to those backups.

Reminders: Study reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device by the operating system. Allowing notifications is optional. The app does not use a push notification server.

Tutorial: The in-app tutorial uses sample data kept in a separate temporary database on your device. It is deleted after the tutorial and does not affect your own data.

Data Security Disclaimer: While we strive to maintain data security within the app's functionality, we cannot guarantee absolute protection of your data from unauthorized access or breaches, especially in cases where your device is compromised or files are exported outside the app.

Third-Party Libraries: We use open-source packages to enhance app functionality. These libraries operate locally and do not transmit data over the internet, to the best of our knowledge.
